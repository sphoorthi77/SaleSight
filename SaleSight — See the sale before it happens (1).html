Every merchant has a hidden layer of near-sales: payments that failed and were quietly retried, products viewed with real intent but never bought, carts that closed one screen before checkout. Individually, each of these looks like nothing — a failure, a bounce, a blip in the logs. SaleSight's Recover module reads them as a sequence instead, and asks a simple question of the whole cluster: does this look like a customer who almost bought, and might still?

ANTICIPATE
What is beginning to change?

A single spike in orders is easy to see — and easy to react to too late. What's harder to see is drift: demand for a category quietly rising week over week, long before it shows up as an obvious trend on a monthly report.

SaleSight's Anticipate module compares each week's behaviour against the merchant's own historical baseline, and surfaces the gap as it's forming, not after it's already happened. Drag the slider to move through six months of the same product category.

SaleSight

The sales you never got to see.

SaleSight treats the transaction as a starting point, not the answer. It reads the signals around a transaction — failures, retries, views, exits, returns, and the timing of all of it — to surface an opportunity a merchant almost missed, then lets them test a response to it before committing to anything.

The system is built around three capabilities. Recover looks at individual customer journeys to find near-misses that are still recoverable. Anticipate zooms out to weeks and months, watching for demand that's beginning to shift before it becomes obvious. Simulate lets a merchant test a decision — a price, an offer, a discount — against that same data before it goes live.

Together they form one loop: see the signal, predict what it means, simulate a response, act, and feed the outcome back into the next cycle.
